# Northwind Legal Aid: Intake Policy IP-140

## Pest Infestation Cases

### Criteria

We accept a tenant's pest infestation case for full representation when any of the following criteria are met:

1. Suspected infestation with signs of active spread, such as droppings, gnaw marks or bites, or a failed inspection such as a city code inspection.
2. An infestation that persists after at least 4 weeks of written notice, with photographs that are dated.
3. Suspected structural damage, a fire hazard, or lead paint.

### Definitions

Written notice includes a letter, an email, a tenant portal message, or a notice left at the management office.

Photographs must be taken within 90 days of the request and must show the infestation.

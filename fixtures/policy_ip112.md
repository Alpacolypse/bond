# Northwind Legal Aid: Intake Policy IP-112

## Repair and Deduct Cases

### Criteria

We accept a tenant's repair and deduct case for full representation when any of the following criteria are met:

1. Emergency conditions, such as a gas leak, sewage backup, exposed wiring, or no heat in freezing weather.
2. A needed repair still not made after at least 6 weeks of written notice to the landlord.
3. Worsening conditions or a serious threat to health or safety.

### Definitions

Written notice includes a letter, an email, or a message through a tenant portal, sent to the landlord or the landlord's agent.

Emergency conditions include a gas leak, sewage backup, and no heat when the outside temperature is below freezing.
